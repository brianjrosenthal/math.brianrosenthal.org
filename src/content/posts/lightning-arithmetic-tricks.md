A guide to arithmetic shortcuts for fourth and fifth graders. Each trick has a page that states it, works two examples and explains why it works, followed by a practice page with a set of problems, a speed round and a "lightning challenge".

It starts with multiplying by 10, 100 and 1000 by attaching zeros, shows that the same idea moves the decimal point in a decimal, and then runs it backwards for division. Later tricks cover squaring numbers that end in 5 and multiplying by 5 and by 11.

A student should know the multiplication facts and place value first. One trick is enough for a sitting.
